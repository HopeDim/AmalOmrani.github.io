I chose the name “HopeDim” as a reflection of who I am: my name, Amal, means hope, and Dim stands for dimension, symbolizing my interest in exploring ideas from different perspectives.

I am a PhD candidate in financial mathematics at Université Paris Dauphine (CEREMADE). My research focuses on dynamic programming methods for computing superhedging prices under transaction costs.

My journey began in engineering preparatory classes, followed by the École Normale Supérieure of Tunis, where I obtained the agrégation in mathematics. I then worked as a mathematics professor at the Preparatory Institute for Engineering Studies of Monastir (IPEIM), an experience I greatly enjoyed. It strengthened both my scientific rigor and, on a human level, my sense of responsibility and admiration for students facing a demanding national exam. It also made me more aware of the subtlety involved in choosing appropriate methods to support each student in their progression.

Meanwhile, I completed a Master’s degree in applied mathematics, working on Gaussian processes for regression and classification, with a focus on reducing computational complexity.
