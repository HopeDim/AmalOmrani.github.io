Amal Omrani (HopeDim)

PhD Candidate in Financial Mathematics • Paris

I chose the name “HopeDim” as a reflection of who I am: my name, Amal, means hope, and “Dim” stands for dimension—symbolizing my interest in exploring ideas from different perspectives, in mathematics and beyond.

I am a PhD candidate in financial mathematics at Université Paris Dauphine (CEREMADE), working on numerically implementable pricing and hedging methods under transaction costs. My research focuses on dynamic programming approaches to compute superhedging prices, with an emphasis on bridging theoretical models and practical implementation.

My academic path began in preparatory classes for engineering, followed by studies at the École Normale Supérieure of Tunis, where I obtained the agrégation in mathematics. I then taught mathematics in preparatory classes, an experience that strongly shaped my approach to clarity and rigor in both teaching and research.

I later completed a Master’s degree in applied mathematics, working on Gaussian processes for regression and classification, with a focus on reducing computational complexity.
